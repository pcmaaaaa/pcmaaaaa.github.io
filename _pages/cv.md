Research experience
======
* Jan. 2025 – Present: **Postdoc Fellow**, Adam Bowman Lab, Salk Institute for Biological Studies, San Diego
  * Developing a new type of fluorescence lifetime imaging microscopy
  * Applying specialized optical imaging methods and molecular voltage indicators for studying neural dynamics in visual circuits
  * Combined training in optics instrumentation and neurobiology; co-advised by Adam Bowman (applied physicist) and Ed Callaway (neurobiologist, National Academy of Sciences member)

* Sep. 2019 – Oct. 2024: **Graduate Student**, Yao Chen Lab, Washington University in St. Louis
  * Optical imaging and computational analysis for neurobiology studies
  * Used in vivo optical imaging system and ex vivo two-photon microscope to study neuromodulator dynamics in the brain
  * Studied brain slice physiology combining optical imaging, optogenetics, and electrophysiology
  * Utilized implants for in vivo bio-signal and sleep behavior assays including optical fiber and EEG/EMG implants
  * Built computational pipeline for imaging system simulation
  * Performed protein engineering of molecular biosensors

* Sep. 2017 – July 2018: **Research Associate**, X.-J. Song Lab, Southern University of Science and Technology, China
  * Chronic pain and gut-brain axis
  * Studied the interactions among neurons, immune system, and gut microbiota in the context of neurological disorders
  * Utilized various disease models including nerve injury, diabetes, cancer, and chemotherapy-induced chronic pain models for molecular pathogenesis studies
  * Developed therapeutic targets for chronic pain including cytoskeleton and amyloid-beta in the peripheral nervous system

* Sep. 2016 – Aug. 2017: **Student Research Assistant**, I. Chiu Lab, Harvard Medical School
  * Neuroimmunology
  * Studied the role of sensory neurons in host-defense of gut bacterial infections
  * Explored the impact of gut microbiota on enteric neuron functions

* Sep. 2014 – July 2016: **Student Research Assistant**, Neuroscience Research Institute, Peking University
  * Molecular biology and pain medicine
  * Studied the pathogenesis of neuropathic pain in peripheral sensory neurons
